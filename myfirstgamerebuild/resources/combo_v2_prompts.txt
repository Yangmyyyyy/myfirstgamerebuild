原有站立/行走/跑步及游戏判定代码未改。以下为实际生成提示。

Reference image: character design and previous attack attempt. Keep all eight knights SAME HEAD/BODY SCALE and facing SCREEN RIGHT. Equal cells with generous transparent margins, weapon never clipped, consistent pelvis horizontal registration and consistent ground baseline; no whole-character resizing. Unlike stiff reference, use convincing whole-body power: bend knees, load rear leg, coil shoulders/hips, then drive through with clear forward torso compression and staggered stance. Preserve crisp pixel art. The force should be visible in anatomy and pose, not only sword angle. Actual transparency outside sprites.
